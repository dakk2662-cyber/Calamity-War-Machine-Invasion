Calamity: War Machine Invasion
This is a small addon for the Calamity mod designed to expand gameplay involving mechanical enemies and to delve deeper into the lore surrounding them.

Current version:
The mod features an expansion of the pre-Hardmode Wulfrum content.

Future Plans:
We plan to add more weapons, items, and accessories in future updates.

Boss/Event battle themes:
Mechanism Warfare — by Digidonix
Wulfrum Invasion Therme — by Spozmotosm
